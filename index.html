THE CORE IDEA

Gradient play, without pretending
the world is flat.

Strategic agents often move on curved state spaces and through restricted, state-dependent actuation directions. The paper develops an intrinsic theory that respects both structures, rather than flattening them into coordinates.

A bundle-splitting condition induces a Riemannian metric under which the averaged interaction becomes a sum of projected gradients.

\(\dot x = \sum_i \kappa_i\,\mathrm{grad}_{E_i} J_i(x)\)

NON-HOLONOMIC GRADIENT PLAY

What an agent wants and what it can do are different objects.

The payoff gradient lives in the tangent space. Actuation restricts each agent to a distribution \(E_i\). The effective strategic motion is the Riemannian gradient projected onto that admissible distribution.

Control-affine interaction\(\dot x=\sum_{i,j} u_{i,j} f_{i,j}(x)\)
Payoff ascent along actuation\(u_{i,j}=\kappa_i\,\mathcal L_{f_{i,j}}J_i\)
Intrinsic target dynamics\(\dot x=\sum_i \kappa_i\,\mathrm{grad}_{E_i}J_i(x)\)

GEOMETRIC DECEPTION

The attacker never touches the victim.
It tilts the geometry the victim sees.

With privileged access to the victim's learning signal, the deceiver embeds that signal into its own input. In the averaged dynamics, this is equivalent to replacing the victim's actuation directions by tilted directions.

THEOREM 3

Stable equilibria persist under small deception.

If the nominal equilibrium \(x^\star\) is locally exponentially stable, then there is an open neighborhood of deception parameters and a smooth equilibrium map \(X^\star(\gamma)\) with \(X^\star(0)=x^\star\). The shifted equilibrium remains locally exponentially stable.

First-order shift
\[\left.\frac{\partial X^\star}{\partial \gamma^k_j}\right|_{\gamma=0}=-dJ_2[f_{1,k}](x^\star)\,(A|_{x^\star})^{-1}f_{2,j}(x^\star)\]

EQUILIBRIUM MANIPULATION

Deception produces a controlled equilibrium displacement.

For the simplex example, the equilibrium shift is exactly linear in \(\gamma\). The geometry makes the manipulation visible: one agent's privileged information changes the destination without destroying stability on the admissible parameter interval.

\(X^\star(\gamma)-X^\star(0)=\frac{\gamma(1-3\alpha)}{3}(1,-2,1)\)

PAPER

Non-Holonomic Gradient Play:

Mahmoud Abdelgalil, Miroslav Krstic, and Jorge I. Poveda

Open PDF ↗

Abstract

We study generalized equilibrium seeking in multi-agent systems whose joint state evolves on a manifold and whose agents act through state-dependent, potentially non-holonomic control vector fields. The framework provides an intrinsic projected-gradient representation, local stability conditions that expose connection terms, and a geometric theory of deception in which privileged information tilts a victim's effective actuation directions and shifts equilibrium.
